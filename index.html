Powder Toy Planetary Chemistry & Reactions

Universal Radial Gravity: Every grain, fluid droplet, and gas molecule accelerates toward the central point (0, 0), forming circular continents, oceanic basins, and atmospheric envelopes.

⚡ Phase 3 Electronics, Logic & Semiconductors:

  • SPRK (Electric Current): Fast-moving spark that conducts through Metal, Mercury, semiconductors, switches, and LCDs, detonating combustibles.
  • INSL (Insulator): Non-conductive, acid-proof, heat-proof barrier that isolates circuits and prevents short-circuits.
  • BATT (Battery): Constant high-voltage power generator that continuously charges adjacent wires and circuits.
  • PSCN (P-Type Semiconductor): Diode anode. Passes spark forward to NSCN and conductors, and turns Switches ON!
  • NSCN (N-Type Semiconductor): Diode cathode. Can ONLY be sparked by PSCN (creating one-way diodes), and turns Switches OFF!
  • SWCH (Switch): Logic gate wire toggled ON (green/conductive) by PSCN and OFF (amber/isolated) by NSCN.
  • DET (Proximity Sensor): Scans nearby space for moving particles, projectiles, or matter, firing sparks when motion is detected!
  • TESL (Tesla Coil): Discharges high-voltage atmospheric lightning arcs (ELEC) into the air towards targets.
  • LCRY (Liquid Crystal): Phosphor display element that illuminates brilliant neon cyan when powered.
  • EMP (Electromagnetic Pulse): Shockwave bomb that electrifies all circuitry in a massive radius.
  • ELEC (Lightning Bolt): Arcing atmospheric electricity that zaps conductors, ignites gases, and fuses sand into glass.
